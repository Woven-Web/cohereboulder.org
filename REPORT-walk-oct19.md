## Not fixed, and why

### Registrant, from the email

1. **The RSVP page doesn't say a confirmation email is coming.**
   - The ecology look cuts `notify.link.rsvpBody` and shortens `rsvpDone` to "You're on the list."
   - **Why not:** it's a deliberate copy cut. Eileen's call.
2. **A second tap on the same RSVP link looks like the first and re-sends the confirmation.**
   - **Why not:** by design. `rsvps.ts` resends so someone can recover a lost confirmation, and the brief says not to change who gets emails.
3. **An email RSVP isn't shown anywhere else.** The calendar still offers a plain "RSVP" on that event.
   - **Why not:** an anonymous visitor has no identity on the site. It would need a design.
4. **An RSVP link for an event that has already started** (for example the 7:30 AM sit, opened at 8) invites the RSVP. The press then fails with "Didn't work. Try again."
   - Read from code (`linkInfo` doesn't check started). I didn't reproduce it.
   - **Why not:** it needs a new sentence.
5. **`/n/garbage` says only "Link not valid."** The body is cut in the ecology look, and the "Notifications" button leads to a page about this device.
   - **Why not:** it's copy. The cut was deliberate.
6. **"Sign in for more" opens the generic sign-in, without the address filled in.**
   - **Why not:** it's flow work.
7. **On an upcoming event, the RSVP form asks for name and email again**, even for someone who arrived from an email that knows them.
   - **Why not:** the token isn't carried to the event page. That's a design change.
8. **The 8 PM email has no viewport meta, and its RSVP buttons are about 26 px tall.** A narrow headless browser rendered it 980 px wide; real mail apps may differ.
   - **Why not:** it's a change to the email.
9. **Site chrome for email-only people.** "Join COhere" is the loudest button on the email-link pages, and the tab bar offers Board, People and Me.
   - **Why not:** design.
